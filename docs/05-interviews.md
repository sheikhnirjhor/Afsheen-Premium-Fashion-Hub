## 3. Interview Transcripts (Key Excerpts)

### 3.1 Interview with Afsheen (Business Owner)

**Q: What is the primary goal of launching this website?**
> "I want to reach customers outside my city. Right now, most of my sales come from word-of-mouth and Instagram DMs. I need a professional platform that represents the quality of my brand."

**Q: What products will be featured?**
> "Premium women's wear — dresses, tops, bottoms, accessories. I also plan seasonal collections and limited-edition pieces."

**Q: What are your biggest concerns?**
> "I'm worried about payment security. Many of my customers are hesitant to pay online. I also need to manage inventory properly so I don't oversell items."

**Q: How do you currently handle orders?**
> "Mostly through WhatsApp and phone calls. It's exhausting. I have two people just responding to messages all day."

### 3.2 Interview with Store Manager

**Q: What are the daily operational challenges?**
> "Tracking orders is a nightmare. We use Excel sheets and sometimes miss orders. A proper system with status tracking would save hours."

**Q: What features would help you most?**
> "Bulk product uploads, order status updates, and a way to see which products are running low on stock."

### 3.3 Interview with Existing Customer (A)

**Q: How do you usually shop for fashion?**
> "I browse Instagram, then visit the website if they have one. If the site looks good, I buy. Otherwise, I DM them on WhatsApp."

**Q: What frustrates you about online fashion shopping?**
> "Sizes are never consistent. I always worry about ordering the wrong size. Also, hidden shipping costs at checkout are a dealbreaker."

**Q: What would make you choose Afsheen's website over competitors?**
> "If they have detailed size guides, good photos from different angles, and easy returns — I'd buy directly from the site."

### 3.4 Interview with Existing Customer (B)

**Q: What payment methods do you prefer?**
> "Cash on delivery, mostly. But I'm open to online payment if the site looks trustworthy and secure."

**Q: How important is order tracking?**
> "Very important. I hate having to message someone just to ask where my order is."

### 3.5 Interview with Existing Customer (C)

**Q: What would encourage you to shop online?**
> "Discounts for first-time buyers and clear return policies. I'm scared of buying something that doesn't match the picture."
