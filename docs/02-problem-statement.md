# 02 - Problem Statement

## 1. Background

The fashion industry is rapidly shifting toward digital channels. Consumers increasingly prefer online shopping for its convenience, variety, and ability to compare products instantly. Traditional brick-and-mortar fashion retailers face mounting pressure to establish a compelling online presence or risk losing market share to digitally native competitors.

## 2. Current Challenges

### 2.1 Lack of Online Presence
The Afsheen brand currently relies on physical retail and social media marketing. There is no dedicated e-commerce platform where customers can browse the full catalog, place orders, and track deliveries independently.

### 2.2 Manual Order Management
Orders are currently processed manually through phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and in-store visits. This leads to:
- Order duplication and missed orders
- Difficulty tracking order status
- Time-consuming inventory reconciliation
- Limited ability to scale operations

### 2.3 Poor Customer Experience
Customers face challenges such as:
- Inability to browse products at their convenience
- No centralized platform for product information, sizing, and pricing
- No automated order confirmation or tracking
- Limited payment options and no secure online payment

### 2.4 No Data-Driven Insights
Without a digital platform, the business lacks:
- Customer behavior analytics
- Sales trend analysis
- Inventory optimization data
- Marketing campaign effectiveness metrics

### 2.5 Customer Support Gaps
Current customer support is fragmented across multiple channels (phone, WhatsApp, social media DMs), making it difficult to:
- Track and resolve issues consistently
- Maintain conversation history
- Provide timely responses during peak hours

## 3. Problem Definition

**How might we build a premium e-commerce platform that provides Afsheen's customers with a seamless, luxurious online shopping experience while enabling efficient business operations and data-driven decision-making?**

## 4. Target Problem Areas

| # | Problem Area | Impact |
|---|-------------|--------|
| 1 | No e-commerce platform | Lost sales, limited reach |
| 2 | Manual order processing | Errors, inefficiency, scaling issues |
| 3 | Fragmented customer support | Poor satisfaction, lost customers |
| 4 | No inventory visibility | Stockouts, overstocking |
| 5 | No analytics or reporting | Blind business decisions |

## 5. Success Criteria

The solution is successful if it:
- Reduces order processing time by 80%
- Increases monthly sales by 30% within 6 months of launch
- Achieves a customer satisfaction score of 4.5/5 or higher
- Handles 500+ concurrent users without performance degradation
- Reduces customer support response time to under 2 minutes

## 6. Assumptions

- Customers have access to smartphones and reliable internet
- Target audience is comfortable with online payments
- The brand has sufficient product inventory to stock an online store
- The team has basic knowledge of web technologies

## 7. Constraints

- Budget is limited to open-source tools and free-tier services
- Launch timeline is 12 weeks
- The platform must be mobile-first (60%+ traffic expected from mobile)
- Payment gateway integration is deferred to Phase 2
